About Novello Stone

Built on the tools, not just the spreadsheet.

Novello Stone is an owner-operated stone and surface solutions business based in Mossel Bay, serving the Garden Route corridor from Mossel Bay to Plettenberg Bay.

Novello is a new business — but it's not new to the work. The name is new. The experience behind it isn't. Years of hands-on fabrication, tiling, and restoration work sit behind every quote we write and every job we run.

We run this like a family business, because that's what it is — no call centres, no account managers, just the people doing the work. Not sure whether something's a fabrication job, a tiling job, or a repair? Ask us anyway. We'd rather help you find the right fit than watch you guess.

The name

Why "Novello"?

Novello means "new" in Italian. It's a new company, built properly from scratch — its own name, its own standards, nothing inherited or renamed. But the person running it has been doing this work for 15+ years. New business. Not a new trade.
