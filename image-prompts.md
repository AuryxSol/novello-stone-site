# Novello Stone — image prompts for GPT

Paste the **style prefix** at the start of every prompt below, then add the
specific shot description. Ask for a photoreal image each time (not
illustration/render) — these are meant to sit alongside the real workshop
and fleet photography already on the site, so they need to read as genuine
photography, not AI art.

## Style prefix (use with every prompt)

> Photoreal editorial photograph, moody and warm, dark charcoal/black
> environment lit with a single warm bronze-gold key light from one side,
> soft falloff into shadow, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, no
> text or logos in the image, no people's faces in close-up (hands/backs
> okay), premium natural-stone-fabrication brand aesthetic, shot on a
> full-frame camera with a 50mm lens.

## Marble page

1. **Macro veining** — "Extreme close-up macro shot of a polished dark
   marble slab, dramatic gold and bronze veining running diagonally across
   the frame, wet-look reflective surface catching the key light."
2. **Quarry / stone-yard** — "Wide shot inside a stone yard at dusk, tall
   raw marble slabs racked vertically in rows, warm rim light catching the
   cut edges, slight haze in the air."

## Engineered Stone page

3. **Manufacturing** — "Interior of an engineered-stone factory floor, a

4. **Slab close-up** — "Close-up of a engineered quartz countertop slab
   corner, uniform fine speckled texture, sharp precise edge profile,
   catching a warm highlight along the arris."
