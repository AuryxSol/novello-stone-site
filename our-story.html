Our story

Built on the tools, not just the spreadsheet.

Novello Stone is an owner-operated stone and surface solutions business based in Mossel Bay, serving the Garden Route corridor from Mossel Bay to Plettenberg Bay.

Novello is a new business — but it's not new to the work. The name is new. The experience behind it isn't. Years of hands-on fabrication, tiling, and restoration work sit behind every quote we write and every job we run.

We run this like a family business, because that's what it is — no call centres, no account managers, just the people doing the work. Not sure whether something's a fabrication job, a tiling job, or a repair? Ask us anyway. We'd rather help you find the right fit than watch you guess.

The name

Why “Novello”?

Novello means “new” in Italian. For us, it marks a new chapter — not a reset. The name is new; the standards were earned over years.

The road to Novello runs through years spent in the stone and surface industry, including the chapters built with Unique Selections and Marble Pro. Each one added something important: an eye for material, respect for the work behind a proper finish, and a clear understanding that clients remember how a job was handled long after the dust has settled.

Novello brings those lessons under one roof. It is owner-operated and family-minded: the people answering the phone stay close to the people doing the work, decisions are made directly, and every project carries our name. There are no layers to hide behind and no passing responsibility from one team to the next.

Where we are heading is simple: a trusted Garden Route business with the capability to take on serious work and the character to remain personal. We want to grow steadily — one relationship, one well-finished surface and one kept promise at a time.
